Who We Are

Founded on September 23, 2020, by Govind Shukla (Founder & CEO), InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 licensed non-profit corporate entity established to drive systemic socio-economic change across India. Headquartered in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh, the foundation has expanded from local grassroots initiatives into an active social movement spanning 28 states.

We recognize that poverty, educational disparity, and environmental decline do not exist in isolation. Real, lasting transformation requires comprehensive social support. InAmigos creates practical, scalable interventions that safeguard human dignity and restore ecological balance.

Govind Shukla — Founder & Chief Executive Officer "Episodic charity gives temporary comfort; systemic empowerment builds self-reliant generations."

The Reciprocal Social Engine: Youth Enablement Powers Community Relief

Unlike traditional non-profits with heavy administrative overheads, InAmigos Foundation harnesses the energy of Project Vikas as an operational human-capital engine. Student interns develop practical career portfolios in digital outreach, research, and design. Their work raises public awareness and mobilizes resources that directly fund on-ground food drives, child literacy centers, and stray rescues. University students gain verifiable workplace experience while directly uplifting communities in need.
